## 🧠 A little about me

I'm a second-year **B.Tech student in Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE-VLSI) at NIT Patna**, passionate about building intelligent, data-driven solutions.

I enjoy working at the intersection of **Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science and Software Development** — from understanding the mathematics behind a model to turning it into something people can actually use.

Beyond coding, I enjoy leading technical initiatives, collaborating with teams, mentoring peers and exploring ideas that challenge the conventional way of building things.

- 🔭 **Currently exploring:** Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP, LLMs and model development.
- 🌱 **Building with:** Python, data science, AI tools and full-stack technologies.
- 🧩 **Interested in:** Intelligent systems, applied AI, RAG and real-world problem solving.
- 👥 **Leadership:** Machine Learning Lead, Web and Coding Club, NIT Patna.
- 🎨 **Beyond tech:** Travelling, exploring new places, painting and experimenting with art.
- 🤝 **Always open to:** Interesting ideas, meaningful collaborations and ambitious projects.
